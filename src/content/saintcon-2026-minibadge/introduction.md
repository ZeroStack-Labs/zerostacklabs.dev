# Intro to the ZS Labs MiniBadge for SAINTCON '26

### Why a Minibadge?

So I had never heard of one of these before discovering that [SAINTCON](https://www.saintcon.org) was a thing earlier this year. Essentially, MiniBadges are in use at a lot of Cybersecurity conferences, although I'm not sure they all utilize the same standard. But, essentially these are a type of trading card between security-oriented folks at these types of conferences. Some you can get from vendors for just going to their booth and talking, some you complete challenges for, and a lot you trade, just like baseball cards when were kids (and if you're like me, I still collect baseball and other types of trading cards, haha!). Looking at some of the images of these collections some folks have and make, I was instantly intrigued.

Now, I originally discovered that SAINTCON existed **_after_** they had sold out (I believe the same day the ticket sales went live), and I had perused information about the [MiniBadge Community](https://saintcon.org/communities/minibadge); however, since at the time it appeared I wouldn't be attending, I didn't pay it too much more attention (although I periodically checked the website and actively stayed on the Discord server hoping to find someone selling a ticket). At about 5 weeks before the conference (just a few days before I wrote this article), I was contacted by an attendee who had a second ticket that they weren't going to be using and they offered it to me at cost - so naturally I immediately swooped it up.

Then, it was a rush to figure out exactly what I needed to do/design/engineer in order to make things happen for a limited run of my first MiniBadges.
